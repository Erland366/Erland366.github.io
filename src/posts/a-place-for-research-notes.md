I’m starting this blog as a place to think through research in writing.
I want to use it for paper walkthroughs, notes from experiments, and explanations
of ideas that take me a while to understand.

I started with language model architectures and training objectives, while keeping
their systems implications in mind. As I worked on those problems, I found myself
most interested in the systems questions: how computation is organized, where time
and memory go, and how to make training more efficient.

That experience has drawn me toward ML systems research, which is the direction
I want to pursue in a PhD. I’m now a Research Engineer at MBZUAI, mainly working
on cross-vendor mismatches between AMD and NVIDIA GPU platforms.
I plan to use this blog to explore cross-vendor ML systems, efficient and
distributed training, and the interaction between algorithms and systems.
My language model background will be part of that perspective.

The aim is to make the reasoning useful to someone else, including the parts
that are still uncertain.

If that sounds interesting, you can follow along through the [RSS feed](/feed.xml).
